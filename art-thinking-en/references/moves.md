# Moves: his habits as reusable design actions

Each move turns A into B, followed by something he actually built. When ideating, apply a few moves to get new directions. When reviewing, use them to turn a sticking point into a proposal.

Moves are not rules. If one doesn't fit, try another, or combine two or three.

## Make people do one less thing

| Move | Turn… | Into… | His example |
|------|-------|-------|-------------|
| Discover it | Things people must type (IP, path, account, format) | The system finds it; people just pick a name | VMCFacial finds the computer on the same Wi-Fi |
| Start on select | "Start", "Connect", "Apply" buttons | Picking starts it; next launch reconnects automatically | VMCFacial starts streaming once you pick the computer |
| Place it | Spoken or typed commands | Pick something up and put it somewhere; the position is the sentence | The Board: you don't say the task, you place it |
| Just ask the AI | Settings screens and manuals to learn | Say one sentence in a chat; the environment installs, features grow | CONNECT: each person's software grows from their own AI |
| Check it at hand | Needing the full setup before seeing a result | Use the least equipment to see whether it moves | VMCFacial: load your model on the phone, no computer needed |

## Make the invisible visible

| Move | Turn… | Into… | His example |
|------|-------|-------|-------------|
| Tune it on the thing | A row of sliders with English names | Tap the part on the work itself and adjust while watching | VMCFacial: tap regions on the 3D face |
| Light it up | Status text, checkmarks, numbers | Brightness, color, shape show state and fitness | VMCFacial highlights what you adjust; the board glows where a piece fits |
| Position is state | Lines of status text | Where something is and what shape it has, readable at a glance | The Board: state shown by position and shape |
| Leave results on the map | Finished work vanishing into a folder | Results hang next to the task that made them | The Board: finished work stays on the same map |

## Give freedom, not limits

| Move | Turn… | Into… | His example |
|------|-------|-------|-------------|
| Suggest, don't forbid | Only here, only this path | Go anywhere; brightness hints where fits best | He rejected "you can only drop on lit spots" |
| Move together | Taking turns, queues, step-by-step waiting | Human and AI act at once; one change and everything re-plans | He rejected "whose turn is it" |
| Keep growing | A list that ends | A board that keeps growing; watch the frontier advance | The galaxy model |
| Two paths | Only a simple mode, or only a pro mode | Everyone gets the easy path; advanced people can open it up | yugo: nodes for pros, Layers for everyone else |

## Protect the work

| Move | Turn… | Into… | His example |
|------|-------|-------|-------------|
| Track it separately | A pipeline that merges or averages away detail | Split the detail that would be lost and handle each part | VMCFacial tracks left and right eyebrows separately |
| Own stage | Performing inside someone else's software, under its limits | Build the critical stretch yourself; you decide how it looks | yugo brought streaming into Blender; CONNECT wrote its own engine |
| From file to stage | Delivering a file | Delivering something that opens and performs | CONNECT: deliver a character that can go on stage, not a file |
| Rigor guards the show | Tests that exist for technical metrics | Each safety net maps to a moment that must not fail in front of an audience | yugo: bones return to rest pose on stop; restore before save |

## Find the meaning

| Move | Turn… | Into… | His example |
|------|-------|-------|-------------|
| Walk downstream | Looking only at your own output | Follow the work downstream to the person who gets stuck | VMCFacial: the stretch after the model is delivered |
| Efficiency into fun | Faster, stronger, more accurate | More fun, better feel, more sense of being in charge | Agent Clan: the joy of directing AI, not efficiency |
| Keep the feel | Copying a game's or metaphor's rules | Keep only the feeling; redesign the rules | The Board: it's chess-like but you don't need to know chess |
| Make it for yourself first | Building for an imagined "user" | Build it for yourself, then hand it to everyone | yugo → CONNECT |
